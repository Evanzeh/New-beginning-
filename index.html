Our Story & Mission

New Beginning was founded on a simple promise: no orphan or vulnerable child should ever be left behind due to poverty, hunger, or lack of opportunity. Working directly inside our local village community, we stand beside children who have lost parents and families struggling to meet basic daily needs.

Right now, our community faces urgent needs. Children walk long distances for unsafe water, classrooms are overcrowded, and bright high school graduates risk losing their future because they cannot afford tuition fees. Together with generous well-wishers around the world, we are raising $13,000 to construct a safe community school, install reliable fresh water access, and sponsor our high school graduates.
